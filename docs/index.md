## About Rejesha Green

Rejesha Green transforms manual and paper-based forest-management processes into a centralized digital platform. It supports community registration, permit requests, illegal-activity reporting, conservation planning, and tree-restoration monitoring.

The platform is designed to improve communication, accountability, and collaboration between forest communities and forest-management authorities.

## Key platform capabilities

- **Member registration** for Community Forest Association members
- **Permit-request management** for forest-related activities
- **Illegal-activity reporting** through digital channels
- **Conservation planning** and restoration monitoring
- **SMS and USSD communication** for accessible user interaction
- **Mobile money payments** through M-Pesa
- **Role-based access control** for different platform users
- **Notifications and reminders** for important activities
